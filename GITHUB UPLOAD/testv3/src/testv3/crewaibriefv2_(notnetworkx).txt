Process Overview: 
This is a section of the P&ID of the carbon capture pilot plant at Imperial College London. MEA (Mono-Ethylamine) is to be circulated around indicated in the blue highlighting. The aim of this loop is to determine the maximum safe pump operating speed J100. There is no heating from heaters H200a and H200b, there is no cooling from cooler C100. The yellow highlighted streams are different bypasses or routes the MEA could take should there be a problem with a valve resulting in a leak.
