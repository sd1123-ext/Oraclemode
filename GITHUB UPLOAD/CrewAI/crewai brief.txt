Pump Commissioning Test 1 – Rig Description

Process P&ID

Process Overview:
This document describes a section of the Process & Instrumentation Diagram (P&ID) of the carbon capture pilot plant at Imperial College London. The system is designed to circulate Mono-Ethylamine (MEA) through a loop to determine the maximum safe operating speed of Pump J100. 

Key operational constraints:
- **No heating** from heaters H200a and H200b.
- **No cooling** from cooler C100.
- **Alternative bypass routes (yellow-highlighted streams)** exist in case of valve failure leading to leaks.

Legend:
- **Blue** – Main Stream (MEA flow path).
- **Red** – Closed Valves (preventing flow through specific paths).
- **Green** – Open Valves (allowing flow through designated paths).
- **Yellow** – Bypass Streams (alternative flow routes, numbered 1 to 11).

### MEA Stream Properties:
- **Flowrate Range**: 0 - 12,000 kg/hr
- **Temperature**: 25°C
- **Pressure**: 1 bar
- **Phase**: Liquid
- **Composition**:
  - **MEA**: 15%
  - **Water**: 85%

*Ideal Flow Condition*: Bypass streams (1 to 11) should have **no flow**, and the only active flow should be through the **main stream and stripper column** within the range of 0 to 12,000 kg/hr.

---

## **Detailed Process & Instrumentation Diagram (P&ID) Description:**
The **P&ID** visually represents the **carbon capture pilot plant**, illustrating **fluid flow**, **instrumentation**, and **control components** in the system.

### **Main Components & Their Functions:**
1. **Pump J100**  
   - Located in the **main stream**.
   - Ensures the continuous circulation of **MEA** through the process.
   - Monitored for maximum safe operating speed.

2. **Filter Z102**  
   - Positioned along the **main stream** before the fluid reaches critical components.
   - Removes impurities from the circulating **MEA** to prevent equipment damage.

3. **Heaters (H200a, H200b)**  
   - Not operational in this process setup.
   - Would otherwise provide heat to facilitate chemical reactions.

4. **Cooler C100**  
   - Also inactive in this setup.
   - Used to maintain temperature control when required.

5. **Stripper Column**  
   - Core part of the system, allowing separation and gas-liquid interactions.
   - Equipped with **multiple manual and needle valves** to regulate flow.

### **Summary of Process Flow**
1. **MEA enters the system** at a controlled **flow rate and pressure**.
2. The **pump (J100)** ensures continuous circulation.
3. The **filter (Z102)** removes impurities before **MEA enters the stripper column**.
4. Various **valves regulate the flow** to ensure safety and optimal performance.
5. **Instrumentation sensors continuously monitor** flow rate, pressure, and liquid levels.
6. **Emergency safety measures**, such as **relief valves and alarm triggers**, provide **automatic shutdown mechanisms** in case of abnormalities.
